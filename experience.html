Research, assumptions, reproducibility and rights

Finite public eight-probe fixture: 256 uniform Boolean residual worlds, 16 affine class members, two workflows; read cost 1 and first workflow use cost 1. Guided world is deliberately inadequate (q0=1, others=0); blind mode samples adequate and inadequate worlds with equal class-status probability, uniformly within each subset. The routine mixture is 60% anchored demand and 40% feature-slice demand; both disjoint layouts are optimal. All simulated choices and calculations are inspectable. No real AI inference, private architecture, telemetry or personal data. Mirroring does not create independent evidence. This is a bounded illustration, not empirical evidence of AI effects.
